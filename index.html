🌟 Welcome to LifeGuard

LifeGuard – Smart Life Organizer is an easy-to-use Android application that helps you keep your important information organized and accessible.

Managing product warranties, keeping purchase bills, tracking expiry dates and remembering everyday activities can sometimes become difficult. LifeGuard brings these useful organization tools together in one convenient application.

Whether you want to maintain household product records, organize important bills or keep track of important dates, LifeGuard makes everyday management simpler.

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to make everyday organization easier by bringing useful tracking and management tools into one simple application.

✨ Key Features

Useful features designed to make everyday organization easier.

🛡️ Warranty Management

Keep track of purchased products, purchase dates and warranty periods. Maintain organized warranty records for important products.

Keep important purchase bills and related information organized in one convenient place.

Maintain expiry date records and track important dates to stay organized.
